# USFD Rail Defect Classification — Indian Railways
## Source: IRPWM (Permanent Way Manual) + USFD Manual (RDSO, 2022 Revised Edition)
## Retrieved: 2026-09-04 | Tier 1 source

---

## Classification system (Need-Based Concept — current)

Modern IR policy uses **two categories** only: IMR and OBS.
(The older "REM" category is deprecated.)

### IMR — Immediate Removal

- **Definition:** A defect that is serious in nature and possesses the potential
  to lead to a sudden rail failure or fracture.
- **Marking:** Three crosses (XXX) in RED paint on both faces of the web.
- **Immediate actions on detection:**
  1. Speed restriction of **30 km/h** imposed immediately
  2. Watchman posted at site until secured
  3. Clamped joggled fish plates provided within **24 hours**
  4. Defective rail replaced with sound-tested rail piece (minimum **6 metres**)
     within **3 days**

### OBS — Observe

- **Definition:** A defect that is not currently critical and does not require
  immediate removal. The rail is kept under observation.
- **Marking:** One cross (X) in RED paint on both faces of the web.
- **Actions on detection:**
  1. Clamped joggled fish plates provided within **3 days**
  2. Rail monitored at specified intervals

#### OBS sub-categories:
- **OBS-E:** Defect within the fish-plated zone
- **OBS-B:** Defect on major bridges or within 100m of their approaches

### Weld defect suffixes:
- **IMR(W) / IMRW:** Immediate Removal — Weld defect
- **OBS(W) / OBSW:** Observe — Weld defect
- **DFWR:** Defective Weld — Removal
- **DFWO:** Defective Weld — Observation

---

## Upgrade rules

- If a defect is detected by **two or more probes** → upgrade to IMR
- If **two or more OBS/OBSW defects** are within **4.0 metres** of each other
  → upgrade to IMR/IMRW
- Classification depends on machine sensitivity settings; once calibrated,
  settings must be locked

---

## Deprecated classification

### REM — Remove (historical, no longer in use)
- Was a middle category between IMR and OBS
- Marked with RED paint
- Under the "Need-Based Concept," simplified to just IMR and OBS

---

## Need-Based Concept
Frequency of USFD inspection is determined by:
- Incidence of defects in the section
- Track conditions (age, rail type, traffic density)
- Not a fixed interval alone
